## Brain alerts explained

**🟠 "real estate is within 1.6% of leaving the portfolio"**
- The alert is real, but the number is stale. At the close the cushion is **1.9%**.
- **Root cause:** the evening Brain refresh is in `daytrade.yml` (close job), step "Refresh the Bot Brain…", with `if: steps.dt.outputs.active == '1'`.
  - On an early-close day, `run()` exits with "market closed today", because at 14:20 the clock says closed and the next open is Monday. So `active=0` and the refresh is skipped.
  - The Brain therefore kept the daily run's 11:03 snapshot.
- On normal days the new step should work. Monday will show whether it does.

## One idea worth testing

**How much should we trust the "if the month ended today" preview with one or two sessions left?**

How I tested it:
- I used the live CORE strategy code (`strategies.Core`, live config) on the repo's 1987–2026 history, in a scratch copy.
- For each of 478 month-ends, I ran the Brain's preview one or two trading days early and checked whether the asset set changed by month-end.
- I used the Brain's own `flips()` to bucket each asset by how far it was from flipping.

| Days before month-end | Preview set = final set |
|---|---|
| 2 | 89% (424 of 478) |
| 1 (today's case) | 92% (438 of 478); since 2012: 158 of 178 |

How often an asset actually flipped with one day left, by the move it needed:

| Move needed | Get in | Drop out |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1% | 31% | 20% |
| 1–2% | 4% | 3% |
| 2–3% | 1% | 1% |
| over 3% | ~0% | ~0% |

Raw percentages flatter today's calm-looking numbers, because volatility is about twice normal. Measured in units of each asset's 1-month daily volatility:

| Move needed (in daily standard deviations) | Get in | Drop out |
|---|---|---|
| 0.5–1 | 30% | 25% |
| 1–1.5 | 10% (4 of 40) | 6% (3 of 53) |
| 1.5–2 | 6% | 0% |

Applied to Monday:

| Asset | Move needed | In daily SDs | Historical rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| US stocks get in | +2.2% | 1.1 | about 10% |
| Foreign stocks get in | +2.1% | 1.3 | about 10% |
| Real estate drops out | -1.9% | 1.5 | about 6% |

**Reading:**
- The two stock cases overlap heavily, since both need a broad rally. A fair overall estimate is **about 80% that the 1 Dec plan stays real estate / commodities / cash**.
- The samples in each volatility bucket are small (30–50 cases), so treat the rates as rough.
- **Suggestion (display only, not a rule change):** the Brain could print the flip move in volatility units ("1.1 daily SDs ≈ 10% historical chance") next to the percentage. That would make the preview's confidence visible.

Script: scratch only (`preview_test.py`), not committed.

## Needs the owner

Fixes the desk is not allowed to make. Items 1–6 from earlier notes were fixed today. These are what is left or new:

1. **Evening Brain on early-close days (new).**
   - In `daytrade.yml`, let the "Refresh the Bot Brain" step also run when the market closed early: run it whenever today was a trading day, not only when `active == '1'`.
   - Alternatively, have the 18:35 UTC daily backup run refresh prices and `brain.think()` after the close, even when the daily run has already happened.
   - Today's Brain understates US/foreign distances by 0.3–0.5 points.
2. **Both close crons can pass the gate (new).**
   - The 18:20 UTC cron is meant to be the summer twin. In winter it is 13:20 New York, but with GitHub's usual delay of about 60 minutes it arrived at **14:21**, inside `--gate-hour 14,15`. The 19:20 UTC run arrived at 15:17, also inside.
   - On a normal day both would pass. The second waits in the concurrency queue until the first finishes (about 15:57), then either exits "market closed" (after 16:00) or runs flatten and report a second time (if it starts before 16:00).
   - That is harmless if the report can safely run twice, but it is untidy.
   - **Fix:** gate on *which cron fired* plus the current UTC offset (the `github.event.schedule` string is available), not on the arrival hour. Or skip if today's report already exists.
3. **The close job now runs the entry loop between 14:20 and 15:00 (new, fragile).**
   - Because the close job starts at 14:20, `run()` enters the `now < 15:00` entry block.
   - ORB entries are blocked only because the close job never loads the trend line (`sma=None`, so plan_pair returns "not trading blind").
   - The skill pack is blocked by its own 10:45 cutoff.
   - It is safe today, but by accident. **Fix:** add `and not flatten_at` to the entry condition at line 346.
4. **Live and the research do not test the same window (new; needs an Alpaca data key to test).**
   - `tools/orb_research.py` allows confirmations until **15:00** (`last_entry="15:00"`). The live morning job only trades until **10:45**, and the close job cannot enter (item 3).
   - So the +0.15R expected under the live rules includes trades that live can never take.
   - **Fix:** re-run `confirmed_trade(..., last_entry="10:45")` to get the honest live expectation. The desk could not, because the 5-minute bar cache is not in the repo and the sandbox has no data keys.
5. **Still open from earlier notes:**
   - `state/equity.csv` and `state/trial.json` record the daily run's 11:00 mark. For example, 25 Nov shows $92,968, against a $94,848 close.
   - The 26 Nov holiday row is still there.
   - Recording the evening close instead, or skipping non-trading days, would make the trial comparison honest.
6. **Cosmetic:**
   - On early-close days the close job logs "market closed today". "Market already closed (early close)" would be clearer.
   - The `daytrade.yml` header says the repo is private with 2,000 free minutes. The repo is **public**, so Actions minutes are not a constraint.
   - Being public also means the strategy, trade logs and the masked account suffix on [[10 - Live Paper Account]] are visible to anyone. Please confirm that is intended.
